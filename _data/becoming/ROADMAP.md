# Becoming Roadmap

`Becoming` is a long-term curriculum joined to a deliberately small weekly practice. The roadmap may span fifteen years; the current page should still answer what matters this Sunday.

## Current Cycle

**Cycle 01 — Foundations** runs from August 30 to November 21, 2026. Its four outcomes are: sit FM prepared during the official examination window; complete a rigorous interpretability-foundations sequence; maintain practical English; and maintain the private physical process. Cycle 02 is intentionally undefined until the Cycle 01 review.

## Active Domains

Actuarial, Research, and Physical are ACTIVE. English, Career, and Capital are MAINTENANCE. Mathematics, Lean, Writing, and Building are DORMANT as independent priorities. Writing, code, notebooks, figures, and software may still be required evidence produced by Research.

## Research

Research begins with a concrete interpretability curriculum: define the object of interpretation, compare interpretable models with post-hoc explanation, reproduce PDP/ICE and LIME, understand Shapley values and SHAP, test ALE under dependence, study failure modes, then unify and stress-test the methods. The cycle ends with a serious technical memo.

That memo is evidence toward **R0 — Research Foundations**, not Independent Researcher. R0 also requires six serious readings, three implementations, a comparative experiment, and ten nontrivial questions. **R1 — Reproducer** requires three nontrivial reproductions and a polished reproduction/synthesis work. **R2 — Investigator** requires controlled questions, sensitivity analysis, assumption testing, comparison, criticism, and Research Work II. **R3 — Research Author** requires complete manuscript craft and Research Works III and IV. **R4 — Independent Researcher** requires five substantial works, ending in formal external validation. Draft, working paper, preprint, submitted, revise-and-resubmit, accepted, published, and peer-reviewed remain distinct.

## Actuarial

FM is the active path. It contains a diagnostic, error classification, topic mastery, cumulative practice, timed practice, error-log mastery, the exam attempt, and the separately evidenced passing result. The official October 2026 window is stored as October 1–12; the private appointment is not invented.

The broader CAS graph records P, FM, VEEs, DISCs, MAS-I, MAS-II, Exam 5, PCPA Exam then PCPA Project, jurisdiction-appropriate Exam 6, professionalism, and ACAS. Exams 7–9 and ACAS support FCAS without pretending every future sitting date is known.

## Mathematics

The dormant long horizon moves from mathematical maturity and proof discipline through rigorous analysis, measure/integration, probability, stochastic processes, advanced applied probability, independent reading, a proof/exposition portfolio, reproduction of a nontrivial mathematical result, and independent investigation. Degrees and passive course consumption are not mastery evidence.

## Lean

The dormant path moves through Lean fundamentals, *Theorem Proving in Lean*, *Mathematics in Lean*, a small proof portfolio, fifty understood formalizations, a standalone project, current contribution guidance, community discussion, a first PR, a merged PR, multiple maintained contributions, and formalization of the user's own mathematics. Code the user cannot explain is not evidence.

## Writing

Writing tracks finished work: polished essays, sustained essay portfolios, serious long-form exposition, technical and research writing, then book thesis, architecture, chapters, complete manuscript, editorial feedback, revision, and actual publication. Drafts are not publications.

## Career

Position and capability are separate. The capability path moves from owning analysis and explaining results to owning end-to-end work, presenting recommendations, seeing a recommendation used, measuring impact, becoming a subject-matter resource, leading cross-functional work, mentoring, and technical direction. Employer-confidential details remain private.

## Building

Building measures useful software rather than repositories: prototype, documentation, tests, release, external user, feedback, issue resolution, maintenance, and several useful maintained tools. No users or adoption are inferred.

## English

English has speaking, listening, writing, daily-life, and professional dimensions. Observable evidence includes sustained conversation, actuarial and mathematical explanations, everyday scenarios, interview, technical presentation, unscripted questions, and a professional work session. B2/C1 certification is never claimed without suitable evidence. During Cycle 01 this is maintenance.

## Capital

Capital accumulation and investing skill are separate. The public roadmap contains policies, systems, and milestone structure; actual values remain private. Market appreciation is not treated as proof of skill, and the system does not reward leverage or excessive risk. A monthly review is normally sufficient.

## Physical

The physical process is ACTIVE but private. It tracks adherence, trend, chosen movement/training consistency, user-defined milestones, physical capacity, and maintenance after a target. It gives no medical treatment or unsafe weekly prescription. Touching a target once is not the final unlock.

## Legendary Achievements

The deliberately small set is FCAS, Independent Researcher, Published Author, Lean Contributor, $1M Investable Assets, C1 Professional Fluency, Actuary–Researcher, and Independent Scholar. These depend on evidence, not node counts or identity claims.

## How Weekly Progress Feeds Long-Term Progress

Weekly quests attach to roadmap nodes and state why they matter and what evidence they should produce. A Weekly Review records only deltas: progress, evidence, bottleneck, discovery, adjustment, and an optional roadmap change. Monthly reviews remove misaligned work. Cycle reviews decide which domains become ACTIVE next. This keeps ambition large while keeping Sunday maintenance small.
